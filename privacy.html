Privacy

Privacy notice

How AtlasMigrate handles your information.

What we collect and why

  • Questionnaire answers, to show suggestions. Scoring runs in your browser. Your answers are kept in this browser tab’s session storage and are cleared when you close the tab. They are not sent to us.
  • Contact details and answers in the referral form, to share with a licensed professional you choose. This only happens if you tick the separate consent box. Online sending is not switched on yet, so the form currently sends nothing.

Sensitive answers

The declaration questions (criminal record, health, visa refusals) are yes/no only. We don’t ask for details, and your answers don’t change your results.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t send questionnaire answers to advertising tools.

Retention

Referral requests will be deleted after 12 months.

Cookies

This site uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, or withdraw consent, through the contact form on the results page.

Applicable laws

We follow the principles of Canada’s PIPEDA (Office of the Privacy Commissioner guidance (official site, opens in a new tab)). Provincial laws and, for visitors in the EU or UK, the GDPR / UK GDPR may also apply.
