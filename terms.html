Terms of use

General information only

This tool gives general information to help you explore countries you might consider. It is not legal or immigration advice and it doesn't assess your eligibility. Immigration rules change often. Please check the official government sources linked here, and speak with a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer about your situation. AtlasMigrate is not a government website and is not affiliated with any government.

No guarantee

Only the immigration authorities of each country can decide on an application. Using this site does not create a client relationship with us or with any consultant.

What the results mean

Results are countries and pathways that may be relevant to your answers. They are not an eligibility assessment, an official points score or a recommendation to apply under any program. Fit bands are broad on purpose.

Official sources

Each pathway links to its official government page, checked on the date shown. Always confirm current rules there.

Licensed professionals

Registered users can chat with immigration professionals whose licence for a country has been reviewed against that country’s official registry. A listing is not an endorsement; you can always check a professional’s licence on the official registry yourself.
